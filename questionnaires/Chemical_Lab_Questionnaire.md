## How to complete

- Answer from records where possible (LIMS, CDS audit trails, logbooks, training matrix). Mark every answer with its source label:
  **[V]** verified from records · **[E]** your estimate · **[N/A]** not applicable · **[?]** unknown.
- Times are in **minutes** unless stated. Always separate **hands-on** analyst time from **unattended instrument/oven time** — they drive different resources (people vs instruments).
- Do not repeat data held centrally (production plan, HR absence, finance costs) — Section B and C ask only for what the lab knows best.
- Where Section E is pre-filled from `Leadtime.xlsx`, **confirm, correct or split** the value; do not leave pre-filled values unreviewed.
- Attach supporting files with the reference shown (e.g. *Att-D1 equipment list*).
- Estimated time to complete: 3–4 hours, plus 1–2 weeks for timed observations in E.4.

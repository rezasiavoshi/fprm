# Finished Product Analyst Questionnaire

**Analyst edition** of the *QC Laboratory Capacity, Staffing Gap and CAPEX Assessment*.

- **Area:** QC Chemical Laboratory — finished products
- **Who fills it:** each analyst testing release and stability samples (2–3 people, one copy each)
- **Working file:** `FP_Analyst_Questionnaire.xlsx` — the tables are only in the workbook; this page lists the questions and explains the sheets.

## How the answers are used

Your answers give the time each test takes and how many samples arrive, which shows how busy the lab and its instruments are and whether more staff or instruments are needed. Times are asked as minimum / most likely / maximum, and hands-on analyst time is kept apart from unattended instrument time. Do not insert, delete or reorder columns in the workbook.

## Workbook sheets

| Sheet | What to do |
|---|---|
| Guide | Rules, which sheets take new rows, and what every column means. |
| Questions | Answer sections A–E below. |
| Volumes | Release batches/yr and stability samples/yr per product, now and future; stability time points; IPC samples. |
| Test times | 385 family × test rows (assay, related substances, dissolution, CU, ID, other). |
| Campaign groups | 13 actives shared by more than one family: can they share an HPLC sequence? |
| Extra tests | Add tests that are missing (blend uniformity, KF, preservative assay, delivered dose). |

## Pre-filled data

- 107 products in 64 method families; dosage forms: 96 Tablet, 7 Capsule, 2 Sachet, 1 Nasal spray, 1 not stated.
- 7 families have no active ingredient in the product formulas (F22, F37, F41, F42, F60, F62, F63); the analyst is asked to name them.
- Families group strengths that share methods, so times are asked once per family, not per product.
- There is no recorded time catalogue for finished products; every time on this questionnaire is a new estimate.

# Micro Lab Analyst Questionnaire

**Analyst edition** of the *QC Laboratory Capacity, Staffing Gap and CAPEX Assessment*.

- **Area:** QC Microbiology Laboratory — raw materials, finished products, water and environmental monitoring
- **Who fills it:** each microbiologist (2–3 people, one copy each)
- **Working file:** `Micro_Analyst_Questionnaire.xlsx` — the tables are only in the workbook; this page lists the questions and explains the sheets.

## How the answers are used

Your answers give the hands-on time of each micro test, how long it fills incubators and cabinets, and how many samples arrive, which shows how busy the lab and its equipment are and whether more staff or equipment are needed. Times are asked as minimum / most likely / maximum, and incubation is kept apart from analyst time. Do not insert, delete or reorder columns in the workbook.

## Workbook sheets

| Sheet | What to do |
|---|---|
| Guide | Rules, which sheets take new rows, and what every column means. |
| Questions | Answer sections A–E below. |
| Test catalog | Time each micro test once (18 tests and monitoring samples): hands-on time, incubation days, plates per sample, cabinet time. |
| Test times | 407 raw material / product family × micro test rows: confirm Applicable and the % of lots tested. |
| Monitoring | Water, environmental, personnel, compressed-air and swab programmes: points, samples per week, sampling rounds. |
| Support work | Media, growth promotion, cultures, autoclave, identification, investigations, qualification: units per year and hours. |
| Extra tests | Add tests that are missing (other organisms, in-house tests, stability-only tests). |

## Pre-filled data

- 180 raw materials on the master list: 30 have a micro test in their USP–NF monograph; 19 more are of natural origin (extracts, premixes, gelatin) and pre-set to Y; the rest have one row set to N.
- 64 finished-product families (107 products): TAMC / TYMC and E. coli for oral solids, plus Salmonella where the product contains a botanical; S. aureus, P. aeruginosa and preservative efficacy for the nasal spray (USP <1111>, <51>).
- Lots and batches per year come from the RM and FP questionnaires; this one asks only the share tested for micro.
- Incubation is asked in days and kept apart from analyst time: it fills incubators, not people.
- Sterility and endotoxin tests are pre-set to N: no sterile product is on the product list.
- There is no recorded time catalogue for micro tests; every time on this questionnaire is a new estimate.

# Raw Material Analyst Questionnaire

**Analyst edition** of the *QC Laboratory Capacity, Staffing Gap and CAPEX Assessment*.

- **Area:** QC Chemical Laboratory — raw materials
- **Who fills it:** each analyst testing incoming materials (2–3 people, one copy each)
- **Working file:** `RM_Analyst_Questionnaire.xlsx` — the tables are only in the workbook; this page lists the questions and explains the sheets.

## How the answers are used

Your answers give the time each test takes and how many samples arrive, which shows how busy the lab and its instruments are and whether more staff or instruments are needed. Times are asked as minimum / most likely / maximum, and hands-on analyst time is kept apart from unattended instrument time. Do not insert, delete or reorder columns in the workbook.

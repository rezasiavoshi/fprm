# Raw Material Analyst Questionnaire

**Analyst edition** of the *QC Laboratory Capacity, Staffing Gap and CAPEX Assessment*.

- **Area:** QC Chemical Laboratory — raw materials
- **Who fills it:** each analyst testing incoming materials (2–3 people, one copy each)
- **Working file:** `RM_Analyst_Questionnaire.xlsx` — the tables are only in the workbook; this page lists the questions and explains the sheets.

## How the answers are used

Your answers give the time each test takes and how many samples arrive, which shows how busy the lab and its instruments are and whether more staff or instruments are needed. Times are asked as minimum / most likely / maximum, and hands-on analyst time is kept apart from unattended instrument time. Do not insert, delete or reorder columns in the workbook.

## Workbook sheets

| Sheet | What to do |
|---|---|
| Questions | Answer sections A–E. |
| Materials | Lots/yr now and future, suppliers, reduced testing - and whether unlisted names are still tested. |
| Test catalog | Time each general test once (LOD, KF, pH, IR, titration ...). |
| Test times | One row per material x compendial test: confirm Applicable; time the HPLC/GC tests. |
| Extra tests | Tests you run that are missing from Test times. |

## Pre-filled data

- 180 materials on the master list (94 Active, 13 Colorant, 10 Flavour, 9 Filler / Diluent, others); 129 have a USP–NF monograph.
- *Test times* lists each monograph test as its own row (1179 rows). General tests link to the *Test catalog* (29 tests), so they are timed once; HPLC/GC tests are timed per material.
- Left out: microbial, sterility and endotoxin tests (Micro lab). Pre-set to N: tests that apply only to some grades (e.g. *where labelled for injectables*). Where a monograph gives alternative assays, keep only the one you run.
- *Materials*: the master list, then 59 purchase-list names with a suggested match to confirm, then 586 names found only in the purchase list or the lab time records.
- Recorded lab times are per test set and include unattended time; they are shown in hours as a starting point. Two columns on *Materials* flag tests in the monograph but not in the lab record, and the reverse; differences are expected where a material is tested to BP/EP or a supplier spec - then fix *Test times* to what you run.

# Raw Material Analyst Questionnaire

**Deliverable 2 — analyst edition** of the *QC Laboratory Capacity, Staffing Gap and CAPEX Assessment* (see `reports/QC_Lab_Capacity_Staffing_CAPEX_Report.md`). The unit-head edition is `questionnaires/Chemical_Lab_Questionnaire.md`.

- **Area:** QC Chemical Laboratory — raw materials
- **Who fills it:** each analyst testing incoming materials (2–3 people, one copy each)
- **Working file:** `RM_Analyst_Questionnaire.xlsx` — the tables are only in the workbook; this page lists the questions and explains the sheets.
- **Generated by:** `tools/build_analyst_questionnaires.py` from `data/QC_Capacity_Study.xlsx`, `data/Leadtime.xlsx`, `data/rawmaterials.md` and `data/Product-Material-Database.xlsx`. Re-run it after the source files change.

## How the answers are used

Every table keeps the column order of the matching sheet in `QC_Capacity_Study.xlsx`, so answers are pasted back without re-typing, and the study's `Capacity` sheet then shows utilisation and the staffing/instrument gap. Times are asked as minimum / most likely / maximum, and hands-on analyst time is kept apart from unattended instrument time.
